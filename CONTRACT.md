# Beam target product contract

Beam is an employee LMS for US employers moving from TalentLMS, Trainual, Docebo or iSpring Learn, with the collaborative, AI and mobile learning expected from 360Learning, Sana (Workday) and Arist. It turns company procedures, licensed libraries and existing packages into maintained onboarding, compliance, practice and renewal programs.

## Product target

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Employee training software / learning management system |
| Incumbents a buyer switches from | TalentLMS, Trainual, Docebo, iSpring Learn |
| Up-and-comers it must also beat | 360Learning, Sana (Workday), Arist |
| First buyer | An operations or people lead at a 20–200 employee US employer with repeat onboarding, multi-state compliance and changing procedures; USD costs and explicit US IANA time zones |

The primary journey: Maintain the workforce directory, author and review revision-bound learning, enroll the right people, deliver web, native and offline learning, assess understanding and practical work, follow up and renew, then retain and export reliable evidence.

## How to read this contract

- This follows the RER contract standard.
- Core, Agent surface and Later are counted separately, with full-target progress alongside Core.
- DONE requires every acceptance outcome to have recorded public-door proof. Only the paid or network edge may be replaced in a test; application delivery, AI and learning results are never simulated.
- Real-world proof is dated in the Field proof register and never substitutes for seed-producible proof.
- ACCEPTANCE.md, docs/CLAIMS.md and status counts are generated from this file.

## Door rules

- **D-1.** Learning evidence stays revision-bound. Progress, attendance, practice, delivery and imported history do not become an assessment pass; a later publication never rewrites earned completions.

- **D-2.** A source permission or license withdrawal stops new retrieval and distribution of that material, including dependent preparation and learning doors; renewed permission does not revive revoked authority.

### Published native learning guarantees

#### BEAM-01 — Configure the employer and staff access

**Requirement.** The employer has a name and IANA time zone. Staff hold Beam author, reviewer, learner, manager and assessor roles and belong to teams; their learning directory follows ORG-001.

**Acceptance.**
1. Through setup, invitation and people forms, name the employer and zone, invite each role, remove access during an active session, restore it and require a fresh sign-in; try the same actions as a learner.

#### BEAM-02 — Keep owned source versions safely

**Requirement.** Owned learning documents and media retain license basis, evidence, provenance and responsible reviewer. Immutable source versions are checksummed after storage and on each read; a learner opens only material cited by assigned training.

**Acceptance.**
1. Upload a licensed procedure and replacement version through Sources, compare stored/downloaded digests, refuse unlicensed material, and try to open an unassigned source as a learner; uploaded script stays inert.

#### BEAM-03 — Publish reviewed immutable course revisions

**Requirement.** Courses have one editable draft; stale saves are refused rather than merged. Submission freezes a revision for its named reviewer or owner to publish or return. Publication supersedes the prior revision without changing it. For an agent, publication carries commitment and external when it exposes content outside the employer; the company's policy decides the call.

**Acceptance.**
1. Edit and submit a course through Courses, refuse a stale save and an unrelated reviewer, publish by the named reviewer’s human form, under the held company policy refuse agent JSON publication and confirm later draft edits leave the published revision unchanged.

#### BEAM-04 — Build versioned role paths

**Requirement.** Role paths list a team's required courses with due days, prerequisites and renewal intervals. Saving a path creates a new path revision; assignments keep the revision they came from. A learner cannot start a course until its prerequisite is completed. Assigning a path twice creates no duplicates.

**Acceptance.**
1. Save and revise a role path through Paths, assign it twice, and attempt the next course before and after its prerequisite; inspect the original assignment’s retained path revision.

#### BEAM-05 — Assign individuals and teams

**Requirement.** Assignments go to individuals and teams with a due date, time and explicit IANA time zone, and bind the course's published revision. One open assignment per learner and course. Invalid dates and zones are refused.

**Acceptance.**
1. Assign a published course through Manage to one person and a team, repeat it, and try invalid calendar dates and zones; inspect one open assignment per person/course with its precise revision and due instant.

#### BEAM-06 — Resume native learning across devices

**Requirement.** Lesson progress is stored on the server; a learner resumes at the next unread lesson from any device or session, at desktop and phone widths.

**Acceptance.**
1. Read one lesson through My learning, sign in in another browser/device session and resume at the next unread lesson at desktop and phone widths.

#### BEAM-07 — Score quizzes and control retries

**Requirement.** Deterministic quizzes: a question scores only when exactly the correct choices are selected; the pass mark is a percentage. Every attempt keeps its responses, score, scoring rule and retry decision. When attempts run out a manager may grant one more, with a reason.

**Acceptance.**
1. Take correct, partly correct and failing quiz attempts through the learner page, exhaust retries, and have a manager grant a reasoned extra attempt; inspect stored responses and scoring rules.

#### BEAM-08 — Decide practical evidence through the named assessor

**Requirement.** Practical evidence (text and an optional file) goes to the assessment's named assessor; only that person with the current assessor role can read and accept or reject it (the learner and managers retain read access), and nothing completes before the decision. 

**Acceptance.**
1. Submit text and a file through Practical evidence, try an unrelated assessor, revoke the named assessor’s role in an existing session, then restore and decide; completion waits for the authorized decision.

#### BEAM-09 — Keep exact revision completion evidence

**Requirement.** Native learning completion is recorded only when every lesson of the assigned revision is done and each required assessment is passed or accepted, and binds the exact course and assessment revision with its evidence. Offline completion uses the separate instructor attestation in BEAM-10. Every completion linked to an assignment must match its learner, course and exact course revision. No door turns partial progress into a completion. Publishing a new revision, with or without reassignment, leaves earlier completions bound to their revision.

**Acceptance.**
1. Attempt premature completion, finish the required lessons and assessments through learner doors, publish a newer course revision and compare the old completion’s exact course/assessment revision and evidence.

#### BEAM-10 — Record instructor-attested offline training

**Requirement.** Managers and assessors record instructor-attested offline training (date, instructor, location, attestation, optional evidence file) against an explicitly selected current or historical published revision; it closes only assignments for the same learner, course and exact revision and records its provenance. Another revision's open assignment stays open; that attendance has its own completion and certificate. Future dates are refused.

**Acceptance.**
1. Record attendance through Manage for current and historical published revisions, reject future dates and verify only a matching revision’s assignment closes while the other remains open.

#### BEAM-11 — Issue numbered completion certificates

**Requirement.** Every completion issues a numbered certificate naming the issuer (the business), learner, course revision, date and optional expiry, stating that it is not an accreditation or a statement of competence. Learners see their own; managers see all.

**Acceptance.**
1. Complete a course, open its certificate as learner and manager, and verify its number, employer issuer, exact revision, date, expiry and non-accreditation statement.

#### BEAM-12 — Work reminders and decision queues

**Requirement.** In-app reminders for assignments due within three days or overdue; manager queues for overdue work, exhausted quiz attempts and renewals due within 30 days (assignable in one action); reviewer queues for revisions and source-change notices; assessor queues for practical evidence. Repeated renewals retain the completed assignment's interval, path revision and prerequisite even after the path changes; each certificate keeps that expiry policy. One latest completion per learner/course is considered, including when offline dates tie, and no renewal duplicates an open assignment.

**Acceptance.**
1. Open learner reminders and each role’s queue with due, overdue, retry, source-change, practical and renewal work; repeat a renewal after a path change and verify no duplicate or lost original renewal policy.

#### BEAM-13 — Protect each learner’s private records

**Requirement.** A learner's assignments, attempts, evidence files, records and certificates remain private to their authorized readers. Another learner's request is indistinguishable from a missing item.

**Acceptance.**
1. Request a second learner’s assignments, attempts, files, records and certificates through pages, downloads and JSON, then sign out and send a cross-origin write; each is refused without disclosure.

#### BEAM-14 — Maintain procedures with scoped preparation

**Requirement.** A source change flags every course citing its earlier version for a materiality decision. Preparation proposals retain the exact draft and source versions, quoted passages and missing information, and apply only while that draft is unchanged. Publication or a binding materiality decision by an agent carries commitment, decided by the company's policy.

**Acceptance.**
1. Under the held company policy, change a source, inspect the reviewer’s affected-course notice, submit a passage-cited preparation proposal, reject an outdated draft proposal, revoke its delegated session and refuse agent publication or decision.

#### BEAM-15 — Move TalentLMS historical records in safely

**Requirement.** TalentLMS move-in kit (records first): course list CSV and course report workbooks (users overview and timeline). The preview writes nothing and reports matched and unresolved people, duplicates, unreadable rows, unmapped courses and gaps (certificate files, course content). Apply is bound to the reviewed fingerprint through Base's D1 import kit with a durable journal and retained originals. Imported completions stay labeled TalentLMS history and complete nothing current. Re-importing the same export adds nothing. Undo removes untouched imported rows and keeps rows a manager reviewed after import.

**Acceptance.**
1. Preview a TalentLMS course-list CSV and users-overview/timeline workbooks in Import, verify no writes, resolve gaps, apply the fingerprint, repeat without duplicates and undo while preserving subsequently reviewed rows.
2. The Move in screen and Business grants share retained upload, plan, fingerprint review, apply, reconcile and undo.
3. Self-hosted Business imports default off; RER's server-owned provisioning approval enables them after the first owner claim and later owner paths heal a missed enable without overriding a disabled door.
4. Durable key revocation survives re-enabling.
5. Owner history and undo include prior imports.
6. Only Move in permits a same-origin static upload script and same-origin chunk connections; no inline, eval or external scripts/destinations are admitted.

#### BEAM-16 — Export and restore the installation

**Requirement.** Installation recovery includes Beam's learning tables, stored media and revision-bound certificates. After a new sign-in the recovered learning journey reads its records, files and certificates. Archive files cannot be placed in public assets.

**Acceptance.**
1. Use rer export, restore into an empty fresh installation, sign in anew and read records/files/certificates; reject corruption, a populated target and an archive placed under public assets; compare the next export.

#### BEAM-17 — Read and export the training report

**Requirement.** A manager reads a training report of every assignment and every completion recorded without one (offline attendance): learner, course and exact revision, status (open, overdue, completed, expiring within 30 days), due time in its zone, lessons done, quiz attempts and result, practical decision and assessor, completion, certificate and expiry. Course, team and status filters narrow it; the CSV download carries the same rows and keeps spreadsheet formulas inert. Learners are refused.

**Acceptance.**
1. Filter Reports by course, team and state, compare rows to the CSV download including offline completions and formula-like fields, and refuse report access to learners.

#### BEAM-18 — Approve prepared assignments and reminders

**Requirement.** Prepared assignment and reminder plans retain the agent, sponsor, exact inputs, reasons, reasoned exclusions and immutable payload digest. Assignment plans bind the selected course revision; a superseding publication refuses stale application. Only still-open assignments receive reminders. Company policy decides agent calls: standard for reversible internal plans and assignments, external for outside contact, and commitment for a binding sign-off. Under the held policy the manager decides the exact saved payload; the decision retains person, time, note and result.

**Acceptance.**
1. Under the held company policy, issue a real preparation credential, propose assignments/reminders through the API, inspect reasons/exclusions and payload digest in Agent work, reject unauthorized or stale approvals and approve exact work as a manager.

#### BEAM-19 — Ask voluntary anonymous diagnostic questions

**Requirement.** Voluntary diagnostic question plans retain purpose, questions, participants and closing date; answers are once-only and an invitee may decline. Answers contain neither person nor time and are released only after closure with at least three answers, in no identifying order. Process recommendations remain separate decisions and no answer becomes a staff rating. Agent question delivery and process decisions carry standard, adding external for outside recipients and commitment for binding sign-off; the company's policy decides their execution.

**Acceptance.**
1. Under the held company policy, approve a question plan through Agent work, have invited staff answer once or decline, close it below and above the anonymity minimum, then inspect nameless answers and a separately approved process recommendation.

#### BEAM-20 — Practice reviewed branching scenarios

**Requirement.** Practice scenarios bind a published revision and only its cited sources, with situations, choices, feedback and rubric. Dangling, unreachable or endless graphs are refused. Assigned learners receive feedback and can restart; practice creates no score, assessment attempt, completion or certificate and other people cannot open private runs. An agent's deployment carries commitment, adding external for an outside cohort, under company policy.

**Acceptance.**
1. Under the held company policy, prepare a source-cited scenario through the API, reject invalid graphs, deploy it as the named reviewer, practice/restart as the assigned learner and verify no quiz attempt, completion, certificate or other person’s run is exposed.

## Target clauses

### People, access and the learner catalog

#### ORG-001 — Maintain the employee learning directory

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** People records carry a stable external employee id, preferred name, work email (optional: a person without one is identified by employee id and signs in under ORG-011), mobile number, preferred language, employment state, manager, role, department, location, hire date and custom fields. Email changes and rehires preserve the person’s prior learning without conflating two employees. Owners and unit learning administrators edit their scoped records; managers read their teams' learning-directory fields and only custom fields explicitly exposed to them. People read their own record, with edits limited to their language preference under LEARN-017; other staff see names only. Directory email never becomes a sign-in identity merge key. Detailed formats and operating rules are in [Learning mechanics](docs/learning-mechanics.md#org-001).

**Acceptance.**
1. Import and edit a US roster through People, including a person with an employee id and mobile number but no email, change an email, rehire an employee and introduce a conflicting external id; show retained history and a conflict requiring resolution.

#### ORG-002 — Delegate administration with explicit rights

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** The owner controls installation, identity, providers, billing and administrator grants. Unit learning administrators manage only their unit catalogs, learners, enrollment, reports and team membership, cannot grant another role or import an outside person, and manage only access windows of enrollments they created. Removing every unit-team membership returns management to the owner; ending the administrator grant invalidates unused invitations. Owners name team managers; managers extend their teams' windows. [Administration mechanics](docs/learning-mechanics.md#org-002) specifies the scope matrix.

**Acceptance.**
1. Grant a learning administrator one unit, manage its courses and learners, then request another unit and provider credentials through HTML and JSON; deny both and revoke the active grant.
2. As that administrator, invite a learner into the unit's team, set its members, set and extend the window of their own enrollment, and be refused another unit's team, another role, an outside person, naming a manager and another person's enrollment window.

#### ORG-003 — Scope authors and reviewers to their content

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** Authors draft and request review only for authorized content; named reviewers publish or return it and choose retraining. Neither role administers people or sees unrelated learner evidence, and delegation/replacement of a reviewer is recorded without rewriting publication history. A one-person learning team works: when the owner allows self-review for a course, its learning administrator may be both author and named reviewer, and each such publication is recorded and reported as self-reviewed.

**Acceptance.**
1. Give an expert one course, coauthor and review it, then try an unrelated course, people administration and evidence download; replace its reviewer and retain both decision histories; with self-review allowed, have one learning administrator author and publish a fix and find it labeled self-reviewed in history, then refuse the same when self-review is off.

#### ORG-004 — Give managers only their teams’ learning authority

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** Managers assign and follow up their direct or delegated teams, approve due-date exceptions, see team compliance and grant reasoned retries. Cross-team reads/writes and practical assessment decisions outside a separate assessor grant are refused; temporary manager coverage has an end date. The owner names each team's direct managers; a team's members are set by the owner or, for a team in a unit they administer, its learning administrator (ORG-002). Managers do not change team membership, so they never widen their own reach.

**Acceptance.**
1. Sign in as two team managers, assign and report within each team, try cross-team access and an assessor decision, then exercise and expire a temporary coverage grant.

#### ORG-005 — Support instructors and read-only auditors

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** An instructor manages only assigned sessions, rosters and attendance; an auditor reads explicitly shared training evidence for a defined cohort and period. Neither can alter assessments, completions, people or policy; access expires and sensitive files remain individually checked. Detailed formats and operating rules are in [Learning mechanics](docs/learning-mechanics.md#org-005).

**Acceptance.**
1. Invite an instructor and a time-limited auditor through Access, conduct a session and download the allowed evidence, then try prohibited writes and access after expiry.
2. As an instructor-only person, try team and course announcements and receive 403; after instructor access expires, announcing and new replies are refused.

#### ORG-006 — Preview bulk people changes

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** Admins upload CSV or XLSX rosters and bulk invite, update, transfer or deactivate selected employees with validation, field mapping, duplicate handling and a write-free preview. Rows need an email or an employee id; email-less rows map username and mobile columns (as TalentLMS’s Login column does) and get ORG-011 sign-in and first-sign-in sheets instead of emailed invitations. Apply reports per-row outcomes, resumes safely and never deactivates missing rows without an explicitly chosen policy. Detailed formats and operating rules are in [Learning mechanics](docs/learning-mechanics.md#org-006).

**Acceptance.**
1. Upload a 200-person roster with duplicate email, invalid manager, a transfer and email-less rows with usernames, preview without mail/writes, apply twice and resume an interrupted batch without repeated invitations.

#### ORG-007 — Offer an organized self-service learning catalog

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-ACCESS-004, BASE-ROLE-001

**Requirement.** Learners search and browse authorized courses by subject, role, skill, language and duration, see required versus optional work, request approval where needed, self-enroll where allowed and withdraw from optional work without deleting history. Favorites and saved searches remain personal. Detailed formats and operating rules are in [Learning mechanics](docs/learning-mechanics.md#org-007).

**Acceptance.**
1. As a learner find a course, self-enroll in an open course, request a restricted one, withdraw and inspect history; unauthorized courses and other learners’ favorites stay hidden.

#### ORG-008 — Separate employee and external training portals

*Tier:* Core

**Requirement.** The employer can run branded customer and partner training portals for customers, partners, contractors and other external learners, with separate identity, consent, catalogs, enrollment, administrators and reports. #### NOTICE-005 — Define notification rules, recipients and messages

*Tier:* Core · *Cites:* BASE-MAIL-001

### Agent surface

**Specialist: learning coordinator.** It maintains reviewed learning, assigns the right work, follows up gaps and measures whether learning improves work.

Inherits BASE-AGENT-001 to BASE-AGENT-013. The company's policy decides every call; Beam presets no disposition.

**How Beam computes each class.**
- standard: reversible internal learning edits, plans, assignments and reads; count affected records.
- external: material is sent or newly exposed outside the employer, including external portals and provider preparation; count recipients and classify the counterparty as known, new or public from current employer records.
- money: a paid model, content, media or messaging request changes employer charges; carry the estimate, USD and provider.
- commitment: a publication, assessment decision, signed acknowledgment, applicability approval or binding intervention sign-off; carry the exact revision and relying party.
- accounts: a call creates an outside provider account or paid resource; carry the provider.
- gated: the outside provider reserves account consent, checkout, verification or personal terms for a person, including a provider response demanding that step; carry provider and step.
- access: staff, units, roles, portal membership, device or application grants change authority; count people and compute widening or narrowing.
- security: protective source, identity, credential, provider or backup settings change; identify the setting.
- destructive: records, source bytes or history are erased without product Undo; count affected items.
- sensitive: a question, evidence file or imported record contains regulated personal data; carry the kind and record count.
- live: an operational connector, automation rule, portal hostname or running delivery setting changes active service behavior; identify the service.

## Boundaries

Beam owns employee learning and its optional customer and partner portals. Slate owns the company knowledge library; Beam connects to procedures and source citations. Willow and connected HRIS products own worker records, leave and employment decisions; Envelope owns payroll. Beam consumes only authorized fields and writes revision-bound learning outcomes through CONNECT-017.

Beam is US-first adult workforce learning, not an accrediting body, HRIS,
payroll engine, school SIS, course marketplace, videoconferencing service or
specialist proctoring business. Payroll sibling: [Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope). Employers choose applicable training with
qualified content/legal/safety reviewers. Library metadata, seat time and
evidence help meet that decision; a Beam certificate never guarantees statutory
compliance, work eligibility or competence. Safety and health-data requirements
are implementation and agreement obligations in LIB-002 through LIB-004, not
scope exclusions. Specialized proctoring or official credentials use qualified
providers and retain their provenance.

Google/Microsoft/Okta identity, HRIS connections, Slack/Teams apps, mobile app
stores, mail, video, model and licensed-content accounts require employer
authorization, provider permissions and any necessary agreements. Beam shows
what is connected, unavailable, failed or awaiting approval. Provider
subscriptions and content licenses are separately disclosed in USD. A buyer
may choose native package support or an authorized player/LRS connection; both
must meet the same evidence and learner outcomes. Beam does not build a new
Zoom, video CDN, payroll product or library publisher to obtain those services.

The earlier Australian pilot
and first-release exclusions do not limit this owner-approved US destination.
The Beam naming collision recorded in the previous contract remains a launch
naming decision to resolve; this document does not rename the product.
