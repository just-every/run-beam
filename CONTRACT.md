# Beam product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Beam is a complete SMB employee LMS that turns
company procedures, licensed compliance libraries and existing course packages
into maintained role-based onboarding, practice, compliance and renewal programs.
It is the product a US employer can switch to from TalentLMS, Trainual, Docebo
or iSpring Learn, with the collaborative, AI and mobile learning buyers seek
from 360Learning, Sana (Workday) and Arist.

This contract describes the product being built towards. The final Status
table separates implemented behavior from incomplete and missing work; a gap
does not narrow the destination. Each clause includes an observable acceptance
journey through screens, forms, JSON APIs, downloads, commands or authenticated
provider callbacks. ACCEPTANCE.md lists the same journeys and statuses. Paid
or network services may be exercised with their real sandboxes, with test fakes
only at that boundary; no application path simulates delivery, AI or learning.

## Product target

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Employee training software / learning management system (LMS) |
| Incumbents a buyer switches from | TalentLMS, Trainual, Docebo, iSpring Learn, 360Learning, Sana (Workday), Arist |
| First buyer | An operations or people lead at a 20–200 employee US employer with repeat onboarding, multi-state compliance learning and changing procedures; employer and provider costs are in USD |

**The whole job.** Maintain the workforce learning directory, author and review
courses, play the employer’s existing packages, enroll the right people from
HR, deliver learning on web and native mobile (including offline), assess
understanding and observed practical work, run live/blended programs, deliver
follow-up and automatic renewals, and retain/export reliable evidence. Named
connections below are obligations, even where provider app approval, contracts,
content licensing or native app distribution require engineering work.

**Products keep their separation.** Beam owns employee learning and its optional
customer/partner learning portals. [Slate](https://github.com/just-every/run-slate)
owns the company knowledge library; Beam connects to source procedures and
learning citations rather than replacing it. [Willow](https://github.com/just-every/run-willow)
and connected HRIS/payroll products own worker records, leave and employment
decisions. Beam consumes authorized fields and writes learning outcomes only:
revision-bound completions go back to the HRIS training record (CONNECT-017).
It does not absorb unowned category gaps or turn into a course-selling platform.

**Evidence and authority.** Progress, completion and competence are separate.
Native/package completion records the exact assigned course and assessment or
package revision with its method and evidence. Imported history is labeled and
does not silently satisfy current learning. Practice, attendance, message
delivery and signatures do not become assessment passes. Published versions
and old completions remain immutable. Reviewers retain publication/materiality
and assessors retain practical decisions; every handler, API, file and mobile
door enforces the current role and data scope. Agents prepare within explicit
grants, never approve their own work or administer people. Uploaded documents,
packages and operational text are untrusted data, not model instructions.

## Published clauses

The original BEAM-01 through BEAM-20 promises remain intact as the implemented
native learning foundation. Expanded target clauses add obligations rather
than claiming these narrower proofs already cover the full target.

### Published native learning guarantees

- **BEAM-01 — Configure the employer and staff access.** One business with a name and IANA time zone, configured by the owner before anything else. Adult staff are invited by the owner through ordinary emailed sign-in, hold author, reviewer, learner, manager and assessor roles, and belong to teams. Removing access ends every session; restoring it needs a fresh sign-in. Roles are enforced on every action and read. **Acceptance evidence:** Through setup, invitation and people forms, name the employer and zone, invite each role, remove access during an active session, restore it and require a fresh sign-in; try the same actions as a learner.

- **BEAM-02 — Keep owned source versions safely.** Owned source documents and media are uploaded with licence basis and evidence, provenance and a responsible reviewer. Bytes are checksummed, verified after storage and on every read; versions are immutable. Learners open only material cited by training assigned to them. Uploaded content is inert: never rendered as HTML or script. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a licensed procedure and replacement version through Sources, compare stored/downloaded digests, refuse unlicensed material, and try to open an unassigned source as a learner; uploaded script stays inert.

- **BEAM-03 — Publish reviewed immutable course revisions.** Courses have one editable draft with version-checked saves (a stale save is refused, never merged). Submitting freezes an immutable revision; only the course's named reviewer (or the human owner) publishes or returns it. Publication uses the private human session and publication form; JSON publication is refused regardless of cookie or body encoding. Publishing supersedes the previous revision; later draft edits never change a published revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Edit and submit a course through Courses, refuse a stale save and an unrelated reviewer, publish by the named reviewer’s human form, refuse JSON publication and confirm later draft edits leave the published revision unchanged.

- **BEAM-04 — Build versioned role paths.** Role paths list a team's required courses with due days, prerequisites and renewal intervals. Saving a path creates a new path revision; assignments keep the revision they came from. A learner cannot start a course until its prerequisite is completed. Assigning a path twice creates no duplicates. **Acceptance evidence:** Save and revise a role path through Paths, assign it twice, and attempt the next course before and after its prerequisite; inspect the original assignment’s retained path revision.

- **BEAM-05 — Assign individuals and teams.** Assignments go to individuals and teams with a due date, time and explicit IANA time zone, and bind the course's published revision. One open assignment per learner and course. Invalid dates and zones are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign a published course through Manage to one person and a team, repeat it, and try invalid calendar dates and zones; inspect one open assignment per person/course with its precise revision and due instant.

- **BEAM-06 — Resume native learning across devices.** Lesson progress is stored on the server; a learner resumes at the next unread lesson from any device or session, at desktop and phone widths. **Acceptance evidence:** Read one lesson through My learning, sign in in another browser/device session and resume at the next unread lesson at desktop and phone widths.

- **BEAM-07 — Score quizzes and control retries.** Deterministic quizzes: a question scores only when exactly the correct choices are selected; the pass mark is a percentage. Every attempt keeps its responses, score, scoring rule and retry decision. When attempts run out a manager may grant one more, with a reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Take correct, partly correct and failing quiz attempts through the learner page, exhaust retries, and have a manager grant a reasoned extra attempt; inspect stored responses and scoring rules.

- **BEAM-08 — Decide practical evidence through the named assessor.** Practical evidence (text and an optional file) goes to the assessment's named assessor; only that person with the current assessor role can read and accept or reject it (the learner and managers retain read access), and nothing completes before the decision. Removing the role immediately removes decision, queue and evidence access from existing sessions. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit text and a file through Practical evidence, try an unrelated assessor, revoke the named assessor’s role in an existing session, then restore and decide; completion waits for the authorized decision.

- **BEAM-09 — Keep exact revision completion evidence.** Native learning completion is recorded only when every lesson of the assigned revision is done and each required assessment is passed or accepted, and binds the exact course and assessment revision with its evidence. Offline completion uses the separate instructor attestation in BEAM-10. Every completion linked to an assignment must match its learner, course and exact course revision. No door turns partial progress into a completion. Publishing a new revision, with or without reassignment, leaves earlier completions bound to their revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Attempt premature completion, finish the required lessons and assessments through learner doors, publish a newer course revision and compare the old completion’s exact course/assessment revision and evidence.

- **BEAM-10 — Record instructor-attested offline training.** Managers and assessors record instructor-attested offline training (date, instructor, location, attestation, optional evidence file) against an explicitly selected current or historical published revision; it closes only assignments for the same learner, course and exact revision and records its provenance. Another revision's open assignment stays open; that attendance has its own completion and certificate. Future dates are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Record attendance through Manage for current and historical published revisions, reject future dates and verify only a matching revision’s assignment closes while the other remains open.

- **BEAM-11 — Issue numbered completion certificates.** Every completion issues a numbered certificate naming the issuer (the business), learner, course revision, date and optional expiry, stating that it is not an accreditation or a statement of competence. Learners see their own; managers see all. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete a course, open its certificate as learner and manager, and verify its number, employer issuer, exact revision, date, expiry and non-accreditation statement.

- **BEAM-12 — Work reminders and decision queues.** In-app reminders for assignments due within three days or overdue; manager queues for overdue work, exhausted quiz attempts and renewals due within 30 days (assignable in one action); reviewer queues for revisions and source-change notices; assessor queues for practical evidence. Repeated renewals retain the completed assignment's interval, path revision and prerequisite even after the path changes; each certificate keeps that expiry policy. One latest completion per learner/course is considered, including when offline dates tie, and no renewal duplicates an open assignment. **Acceptance evidence:** Open learner reminders and each role’s queue with due, overdue, retry, source-change, practical and renewal work; repeat a renewal after a path change and verify no duplicate or lost original renewal policy.

- **BEAM-13 — Protect each learner’s private records.** A learner cannot read another person's assignments, attempts, evidence files, records or certificates (answered as not found). Signed-out requests get nothing private. Cross-origin posts are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Request a second learner’s assignments, attempts, files, records and certificates through pages, downloads and JSON, then sign out and send a cross-origin write; each is refused without disclosure.

- **BEAM-14 — Maintain procedures with scoped preparation.** Procedure maintenance without a model account: a new source version flags every course whose published lessons cite an earlier version for its reviewer, who records whether the change is material. The owner's agent uses a preparation-only credential delegated from a live ordinary human session of an author, reviewer or manager: current access and roles, session revocation and expiry apply on every request, and it cannot publish, decide, administer or use human forms. A proposed draft (from a person, or the owner's own agent through the API) records the draft version and source versions it was prepared from, the source passages it relies on (each quoting one of those inputs) and the information it found missing; the reviewer sees both. It applies only to that unchanged draft and never to a published revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Change a source, inspect the reviewer’s affected-course notice, submit a passage-cited preparation proposal, reject an outdated draft proposal, revoke its delegated session and refuse agent publication or decision.

- **BEAM-15 — Move TalentLMS historical records in safely.** TalentLMS move-in kit (records first): course list CSV and course report workbooks (users overview and timeline). The preview writes nothing and reports matched and unresolved people, duplicates, unreadable rows, unmapped courses and gaps (certificate files, course content). Apply is bound to the reviewed fingerprint through Base's D1 import kit with a durable journal and retained originals. Imported completions stay labelled TalentLMS history and complete nothing current. Re-importing the same export adds nothing. Undo removes untouched imported rows and keeps rows a manager reviewed after import. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview a TalentLMS course-list CSV and users-overview/timeline workbooks in Import, verify no writes, resolve gaps, apply the fingerprint, repeat without duplicates and undo while preserving subsequently reviewed rows.

- **BEAM-16 — Export and restore the installation.** `rer export` writes every Beam table and stored file to an owner-only archive without sessions or credentials; import restores it into an empty fresh clone, refusing corrupt or non-empty targets; a second export is identical; records, files and certificates read back after a new sign-in. Export refuses to write into `public/`, and a build refuses to ship an archive copied there. **Acceptance evidence:** Use rer export, restore into an empty fresh installation, sign in anew and read records/files/certificates; reject corruption, a populated target and an archive placed under public assets; compare the next export.

- **BEAM-17 — Read and export the training report.** A manager reads a training report of every assignment and every completion recorded without one (offline attendance): learner, course and exact revision, status (open, overdue, completed, expiring within 30 days), due time in its zone, lessons done, quiz attempts and result, practical decision and assessor, completion, certificate and expiry. Course, team and status filters narrow it; the CSV download carries the same rows and keeps spreadsheet formulas inert. Learners are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Filter Reports by course, team and state, compare rows to the CSV download including offline completions and formula-like fields, and refuse report access to learners.

- **BEAM-18 — Approve prepared assignments and reminders.** The owner's agent prepares targeted assignment plans and reminder drafts for a manager's approval. Every proposal records the agent, the person whose credential it used, its inputs (source versions, course or path revisions, assignments, question plans), reasons, exclusions with reasons and its exact payload with that payload's SHA-256. Nothing reaches staff or assignments before a human approves; the agent cannot decide, people without the kind's authority cannot decide, and an approval names the payload it reviewed. Approval records who, when, the note and the result. An assignment plan binds the course revision it names and is refused, staying undecided, once a newer revision is published; approved reminders appear in-app to the learner of a still-open assignment only. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a real preparation credential, propose assignments/reminders through the API, inspect reasons/exclusions and payload digest in Agent work, reject unauthorized or stale approvals and approve exact work as a manager.

- **BEAM-19 — Ask voluntary anonymous diagnostic questions.** Diagnose before assigning: the owner's agent prepares a question plan (purpose, questions, participants, closing date) and a manager approves it before anyone is asked. Answering is optional and once only; an invitee may decline. Answers are stored without the person or time; managers and their agent see participation counts, and the answers only after the plan closes with at least three answers, in no particular order. A process-change recommendation drawn from it is recorded when the manager approves it; Beam never turns answers into ratings. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a question plan through Agent work, have invited staff answer once or decline, close it below and above the anonymity minimum, then inspect nameless answers and a separately approved process recommendation.

- **BEAM-20 — Practise reviewed branching scenarios.** The owner's agent prepares a text practice scenario for a published revision: situations, choices, feedback and a rubric, citing only sources the revision's lessons cite; a scenario with a dangling or unreachable step, or no ending, is refused. Only the course's named reviewer (or the owner) deploys it. A learner assigned that revision practises it from the course page, sees feedback after each choice and can start again; practice is never scored, never creates attempts, completions or certificates, and nobody else can open their runs. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a source-cited scenario through the API, reject invalid graphs, deploy it as the named reviewer, practise/restart as the assigned learner and verify no quiz attempt, completion, certificate or other person’s run is exposed.

## Target clauses

### People, access and the learner catalog

- **ORG-001 — Maintain the employee learning directory.** People records carry a stable external employee id, preferred name, work email (optional: a person without one is identified by employee id and signs in under ORG-011), mobile number, preferred language, employment state, manager, role, department, location, hire date and custom fields. Email changes and rehires preserve the person’s prior learning without conflating two employees. **Acceptance evidence:** Import and edit a US roster through People, including a person with an employee id and mobile number but no email, change an email, rehire an employee and introduce a conflicting external id; show retained history and a conflict requiring resolution.

- **ORG-002 — Delegate administration with explicit rights.** The owner controls installation, identity, providers, billing and administrator grants. A learning administrator manages the catalog, enrollment and reports within assigned business units, without gaining owner/provider authority; grants, expiry and revocation apply in every page, API and download. **Acceptance evidence:** Grant a learning administrator one unit, manage its courses and learners, then request another unit and provider credentials through HTML and JSON; deny both and revoke the active grant.

- **ORG-003 — Scope authors and reviewers to their content.** Authors draft and request review only for authorized content; named reviewers publish or return it and choose retraining. Neither role administers people or sees unrelated learner evidence, and delegation/replacement of a reviewer is recorded without rewriting publication history. A one-person learning team works: when the owner allows self-review for a course, its learning administrator may be both author and named reviewer, and each such publication is recorded and reported as self-reviewed. **Acceptance evidence:** Give an expert one course, coauthor and review it, then try an unrelated course, people administration and evidence download; replace its reviewer and retain both decision histories; with self-review allowed, have one learning administrator author and publish a fix and find it labelled self-reviewed in history, then refuse the same when self-review is off.

- **ORG-004 — Give managers only their teams’ learning authority.** Managers assign and follow up their direct or delegated teams, approve due-date exceptions, see team compliance and grant reasoned retries. Cross-team reads/writes and practical assessment decisions outside a separate assessor grant are refused; temporary manager coverage has an end date. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as two team managers, assign and report within each team, try cross-team access and an assessor decision, then exercise and expire a temporary coverage grant.

- **ORG-005 — Support instructors and read-only auditors.** An instructor manages only assigned sessions, rosters and attendance; an auditor reads explicitly shared training evidence for a defined cohort and period. Neither can alter assessments, completions, people or policy; access expires and sensitive files remain individually checked. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite an instructor and a time-limited auditor through Access, conduct a session and download the allowed evidence, then try prohibited writes and access after expiry.

- **ORG-006 — Preview bulk people changes.** Admins upload CSV or XLSX rosters and bulk invite, update, transfer or deactivate selected employees with validation, field mapping, duplicate handling and a write-free preview. Rows need an email or an employee id; email-less rows map username and mobile columns (as TalentLMS’s Login column does) and get ORG-011 sign-in and first-sign-in sheets instead of emailed invitations. Apply reports per-row outcomes, resumes safely and never deactivates missing rows without an explicitly chosen policy. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a 200-person roster with duplicate email, invalid manager, a transfer and email-less rows with usernames, preview without mail/writes, apply twice and resume an interrupted batch without repeated invitations.

- **ORG-007 — Offer an organized self-service learning catalog.** Learners search and browse authorized courses by subject, role, skill, language and duration, see required versus optional work, request approval where needed, self-enroll where allowed and withdraw from optional work without deleting history. Favorites and saved searches remain personal. **Acceptance evidence:** As a learner find a course, self-enroll in an open course, request a restricted one, withdraw and inspect history; unauthorized courses and other learners’ favorites stay hidden.

- **ORG-008 — Separate employee and external training portals.** The employer can run branded customer and partner training portals with separate identity, consent, catalogs, enrollment, administrators and reports. Sharing an approved course revision does not share employee rosters, private procedures or cross-portal progress; employees remain the first buyer’s main workflow. Each external portal carries its own ORG-009 theme and hostname. **Acceptance evidence:** Create employee, customer and partner portals, share one course, enroll external learners and test every cross-portal page/API/download and administrator grant for isolation.

- **ORG-009 — Brand the employee portal on the employer’s own address.** The owner sets the employee portal’s theme: logo, primary and accent colours, favicon, sign-in page heading and text, and email sender name. The theme applies to every learner and admin web page, the native apps (LEARN-004), every system email and notification (NOTICE-001, NOTICE-005) and certificates (LEARN-002). The owner also attaches a custom hostname such as `training.example.com`. A setup check verifies DNS, TLS and sign-in callbacks before switching, and the previous address keeps redirecting so printed links and QR codes still work. A theme whose colours fail the LEARN-011 contrast checks is refused with the failing pair named. **Acceptance evidence:** Through Settings, upload a logo, set colours and a favicon and attach a custom hostname; watch the setup check fail on missing DNS, then pass. Sign in at that hostname and see the theme on the learner page, a delivered reminder email and a downloaded certificate. Follow an old assignment link and land on the same assignment. Try a low-contrast colour pair and see it refused.

- **ORG-010 — Define custom administrator roles.** Besides the fixed roles, the owner defines named custom roles from checkbox permissions (courses, people, enrollment, reports, live sessions, certificates, integrations, notifications) limited to chosen teams, locations or business units. A custom role never includes owner authority, provider secrets or the named-reviewer and named-assessor decisions, which stay personal grants. Changing a role applies to every holder on their next request, and every grant and change is audited (OPS-001). **Acceptance evidence:** Create a “Site training coordinator” role with enrollment and reports for one location, grant it, then enroll and report in that location. Try another location, a provider credential and a publication, and see each refused. Remove the reports permission and see the open session lose report access.

- **ORG-011 — Sign in without a work email.** Frontline staff with no company mailbox are full Beam learners. A person can exist with no email address, identified by their employee id (ORG-001), whether added by hand, by roster upload (ORG-006), by a file feed (CONNECT-019) or by the HRIS (AUTO-002). The owner switches each sign-in method on or off: an admin-issued username with a password or a numeric PIN of at least six digits, which the person sets on first use from a one-time initial secret; and a one-time code sent by SMS to a verified mobile number from the roster, through the employer’s SMS provider such as Twilio. Both methods work on the web and in the native apps (LEARN-004), and open assignment links from SMS and in-app notices (NOTICE-001, NOTICE-005, LEARN-012). An admin, or a manager for their own team (ORG-004), resets a forgotten password or PIN by issuing a new one-time initial secret; each reset ends the person’s sessions and records who reset whom and when (OPS-001). Failed attempts are rate limited per person and per device; repeated failures lock the account until a cool-down passes or an admin or manager unlocks it. SMS codes expire after 10 minutes by default, which the owner can set between 2 and 15 minutes, and work once. Deactivation, removed access and AUTO-002 leavers end every web and app session, as BEAM-01 does for emailed sign-in. Passwords and PINs are stored only as salted slow hashes, are never shown after issue, and never appear in exports (BEAM-16, OPS-002) or agent reads. Each new hire gets a printable first-sign-in sheet in their LEARN-017 language with the portal address, username, a QR code that opens sign-in with the username filled in, and the initial secret, which expires after a set number of days. A tablet the owner registers as a shared device, such as a break-room tablet, offers quick switching by username and PIN, signs the person out after inactivity or when they leave a course, remembers nobody and keeps no offline downloads (LEARN-005). A person without email never gets email: notices go in-app and by SMS where they have a verified number and have not opted out (LEARN-012); with neither route, the manager’s queue shows “no contact route” for that person (NOTICE-004), never a silent drop. The owner and learning administrators (ORG-002) keep emailed or workplace sign-in for owner recovery; learners, managers, assessors and instructors may use either method. **Acceptance evidence:** Through People, import a roster row with an employee id, a mobile number and no email, and see AUTO-002 create the person and enrol them in their role path. Print their first-sign-in sheet and open its QR code. Sign in on the web and in the iOS and Android apps once with the username and first-use password and once with an SMS code; complete an assigned quiz and download the certificate. As their manager, reset the password, find the reset in Audit (OPS-001) and see the earlier session ended. Exceed the failed-attempt limit and see the lockout, then unlock it. Deactivate the person and see the app session end. Trigger an overdue reminder and see SMS and in-app delivery; remove the mobile number and see “no contact route” in the manager’s queue. On a registered shared tablet, sign in two people one after the other by username and PIN and see the first signed out with nothing of theirs left on the device. Let an SMS code pass its expiry and see it refused. Switch the SMS method off and see SMS codes refused.

- **ORG-012 — View Beam as a learner, read-only.** To troubleshoot, a learning administrator (ORG-002, ORG-010) or a manager for their own team opens “View as” for one learner within their authority and sees the learner pages exactly as that person sees them: assignments, progress, attempts and results, certificates, notices and language. The view is read-only: every write, form, download of the learner’s uploaded evidence and sign-in action is refused, and a banner names whose view it is. It never shows BEAM-19 anonymous answers or BEAM-20 private practice runs, cannot view the owner or another administrator, and does not move the learner’s progress or last-seen state. Each session records who viewed whom, when and for how long (OPS-001) and ends after a short timeout or on exit. **Acceptance evidence:** As a learning administrator, open “View as” for a learner in your unit and see their assignments, progress, quiz results and certificates as they see them; try to answer a quiz, mark a lesson read and change their language and see each refused. Find the session in Audit. Try “View as” for a learner in another unit, for the owner and for another administrator and see each refused; confirm the learner’s practice runs and anonymous answers are not shown.

### Authoring and content maintenance

- **AUTHOR-001 — Author multimedia courses without rebuilding files.** Authors compose reusable lesson blocks for rich text, images, audio, video, documents, links and knowledge checks, preview as a learner, reorder or duplicate blocks and retain source attribution. Web-content blocks embed YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Google Slides, Google Docs and Microsoft 365 (PowerPoint, Word, Forms) links in a sandboxed frame from an owner-editable allowlist; any other embed or active HTML is refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and preview a mixed-media course through the editor, reuse a block, rearrange it and publish; embed a YouTube video, a Loom recording and a Google Slides deck and play each as a learner; try active HTML and an unlisted embed host and confirm the lesson and assets render safely.

- **AUTHOR-002 — Turn existing SOP documents into editable learning.** Import DOCX, PDF, PPTX, HTML and plain-text procedures, preserving headings, tables, accessible images, provenance and extraction warnings. Scans use OCR with uncertain passages marked; the author checks extracted material before it becomes draft lessons. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a procedure containing tables and an image plus a scanned PDF through Sources, compare extracted passages to originals, correct an OCR warning and publish only after review.

- **AUTHOR-003 — Collect expert feedback before publication.** Experts coauthor with conflict detection, comment on specific lessons and questions, resolve review threads and request changes. Learner feedback and broken-content reports reach the responsible expert without disclosing private attempts to all authors. **Acceptance evidence:** Have two authors edit the same lesson, resolve the conflict, leave and resolve a question comment, then submit learner feedback and show it in the owner’s content queue.

- **AUTHOR-004 — Compare and restore earlier content safely.** Authors compare source, lesson and assessment revisions and restore an earlier revision into a new draft. Review and publication create a new immutable version; restoration never changes previous attempts, assignments or completions. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish two revisions, compare them and restore the first into a new draft through course history; publish that draft and verify all three revisions and prior completion evidence.

- **AUTHOR-005 — Organize and reuse the course estate.** Admins maintain folders, tags, learning objectives, languages, duration, owners, templates and reusable courses. Clone, bulk retag, archive and retire keep assignments/history readable and stop retired content being newly assigned. **Acceptance evidence:** Build courses from a template, clone and bulk tag them, retire one with an open assignment and verify retained evidence plus refusal of new enrollment.

- **AUTHOR-006 — Schedule content upkeep and accountable review.** Each course has a responsible owner, next review date and fallback owner. Scheduled stale-content reminders and source-change notices reach reviewers, escalate when unowned and retain an explicit material/no-change decision and next review date. **Acceptance evidence:** Set a review date, advance the scheduler with no source change, remove the owner and inspect fallback escalation; separately replace a cited source and record a no-change decision.

- **AUTHOR-007 — Generate source-grounded course drafts with AI.** Using the employer’s authorized model account, authors ask Beam to draft outlines, objectives, lessons and quizzes from selected company documents. Drafts cite passages, flag unsupported claims and missing facts, show model/cost and remain editable; only the named human reviewer publishes. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a model account, select a procedure and request a course draft through the editor; inspect citations and missing facts, edit a question, refuse injected instructions and publish only by human review.

- **AUTHOR-008 — Translate learning with human language review.** AI-assisted translation preserves course structure, captions, glossary, question correctness and source links across language versions. A language reviewer approves each locale; a source change flags affected translations and old localized completions keep their revision. **Acceptance evidence:** Translate an English course into Spanish using an approved glossary, correct a mistranslation before publication, change the English source and inspect the locale maintenance queue. A learner whose LEARN-017 language is Spanish receives the Spanish version.

- **AUTHOR-009 — Compose conversational microlearning sequences.** Authors split reviewed learning into short text/media modules with questions, branching feedback, scheduled spacing and channel-specific previews. Programs deliver in the employee’s work window, allow pause/resume and maintain a source-cited course revision; shortening delivery never removes a required assessment. **Acceptance evidence:** Build and review a five-day sequence, preview its SMS and Teams rendering, answer a branching question and pause/resume; inspect the same revision and required assessment in web and messaging.

- **AUTHOR-010 — Carry published fixes to learners in progress.** When the named reviewer publishes a new revision, they choose for the course’s open assignments: keep each on its revision, or move them to the new one. Moving keeps progress on lessons whose content is unchanged, reopens changed or added lessons, and voids unfinished attempts on changed assessments while keeping them in history. Passed attempts on unchanged assessments carry over. Each moved assignment records both revisions, who moved it and when, and its eventual completion binds the revision actually completed (BEAM-09). Finished learners keep their original completion untouched. When the reviewer marks the change material (BEAM-14), they may also create new assignments for finished learners, AUTO-004-style, with a chosen due date; this never rewrites or revokes the old completion or certificate. The preview before publishing shows who moves, which lessons reopen and who gets a new assignment. **Acceptance evidence:** With one learner mid-course and one finished, publish a typo fix and choose “move”: the mid-course learner keeps their progress at the same place. Then publish a revision with an added lesson, marked material, choosing “move” and “reassign finished”: the mid-course learner sees only the new lesson reopened, the finished learner’s original completion and certificate are unchanged, and a new assignment appears for them. Repeat the publication with “keep” and see no assignment move or new assignment.

### Course packages and learning standards

- **PACK-001 — Import and play SCORM 1.2 courses.** Admins upload SCORM 1.2 ZIP packages with imsmanifest.xml and assets, validate the manifest and launch path, preview, then assign an immutable package revision. Playback records lesson status, score, time, suspend data and bookmark correctly; completion follows the package’s declared completion rules and remains distinct from competence. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a real licensed SCORM 1.2 package through Courses, preview and assign it, suspend on one device and resume on another, fail then pass and inspect raw runtime events and the exact completion revision.

- **PACK-002 — Play SCORM 2004 with sequencing and separate outcomes.** SCORM 2004 2nd, 3rd and 4th Edition packages retain sequencing/navigation, objectives, interactions, suspend state, score and separate completion/success states. ## Push further

Agents serve the learning job using authorized sources and operational evidence. Every proposal retains its exact inputs, uncertainty, human decision and outcome; publication and assessment remain human decisions.

### Agent work beyond course generation

- **AGENT-001 — Connect work errors to reviewed learning sources.** With manager-approved data scopes, the agent joins procedure versions, learning/attempt evidence and operational error patterns from authorized Zendesk, Jira, Salesforce Service Cloud or owner-supplied CSV/API records. It minimizes/redacts customer and employee detail, cites exact inputs and surfaces missing/conflicting signals before proposing a diagnosis. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize a scoped support/error feed, select a training cohort and request diagnosis; inspect source ids/redactions, reject an out-of-scope record and revoke the connection without retaining unauthorized searchable text.

- **AGENT-002 — Distinguish training gaps from broken processes.** The agent compares observed error patterns, procedure clarity, prior completions and staff questions to propose training, clearer instructions, a system/process fix or no intervention, with reasons and uncertainty. A manager approves voluntary questions before delivery and reviewers keep publication/assessment decisions; anonymous answers never become staff ratings. **Acceptance evidence:** Give the agent one genuine knowledge gap and one unusable process with trained staff, review its cited alternatives, approve a voluntary question plan and reject an unsupported training recommendation.

- **AGENT-003 — Propose the smallest useful intervention.** From a reviewed diagnosis the agent proposes a short clarification, targeted practice, an assessor session, a limited cohort assignment or a process change, with expected benefit, affected roles, paid-time effort and exclusions. The manager approves the exact plan and a content reviewer publishes changes; the agent cannot choose broader mandatory training silently. **Acceptance evidence:** Review an error diagnosis affecting one role, inspect a minimal intervention against a broad-course alternative and approve only the selected people/actions; reject a changed payload and inspect decision lineage.

- **AGENT-004 — Maintain only learning affected by a procedure change.** The agent compares immutable procedure versions to cited lesson passages, quiz answers, practice rubrics and package metadata, identifies invalidated teaching and affected roles/renewal dates, and drafts only necessary changes. The named reviewer decides materiality, publication and retraining; earlier evidence remains immutable and stale proposals are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Change one procedure step, request an impact proposal and inspect affected passages/questions/roles versus untouched content; decline retraining, then approve a revised exact proposal and compare old completions.

- **AGENT-005 — Measure whether learning transfers into work.** Before an intervention, the manager chooses operational outcome, comparison period, cohort exposure, baseline and privacy bounds. The agent compares before/after outcomes and a suitable comparison group when available, showing missing data, sample limits, confounders and uncertainty; completion proves neither competence nor causation. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a metric and baseline through the intervention page, import scoped pre/post outcomes with missing rows and a process change, then inspect the comparison and refusal to assert causation or rank employees.

- **AGENT-006 — Recover overdue learning through the real manager.** The agent uses current HR manager/leave data, assignment deadlines, message delivery and assessor availability to identify blockers and propose a new slot, temporary owner or reasoned deadline exception. Managers approve policy exceptions and escalation; the agent never passes an assessment, backdates evidence or instructs unpaid work. **Acceptance evidence:** Present an overdue learner on leave with bounced mail and a full assessor calendar, review the agent’s blocked-work plan and approve a substitute slot/manager; inspect delivery and retained original dates.

- **AGENT-007 — Prepare a verified migration and maintenance plan.** An agent reads an owner-authorized incumbent export, proposes identity/course mappings and identifies missing assets, package incompatibility, stale content and review owners before cutover. It cites every source object, estimates human work and provider cost, and cannot apply migration, publish replacements or discard unresolved evidence without the responsible human decision. **Acceptance evidence:** Give the agent a real authorized export with ambiguous identities and missing media, inspect object-cited mappings and unresolved work, edit the plan and approve it through the normal fingerprinted importer.

- **AGENT-008 — Evaluate and retire ineffective interventions.** After an approved measurement window the agent compares intended and observed outcomes, identifies non-improving training or changed process conditions and proposes revised practice, a process fix or retirement of redundant future requirements. Humans approve any policy/content change; negative findings remain visible and prior completions are never erased to make the result look better. **Acceptance evidence:** Close a measured intervention with no observed improvement, review the agent’s uncertainty and alternatives, retire only future redundant assignments by manager approval and verify unchanged historical outcomes and evidence.

## Honest boundaries

Beam is US-first adult workforce learning, not an accrediting body, HRIS,
payroll engine, school SIS, course marketplace, videoconferencing service or
specialist proctoring business. Employers choose applicable training with
qualified content/legal/safety reviewers. Library metadata, seat time and
evidence help meet that decision; a Beam certificate never guarantees statutory
compliance, work eligibility or competence. Safety and health-data requirements
are implementation and agreement obligations in LIB-002 through LIB-004, not
scope exclusions. Specialized proctoring or official credentials use qualified
providers and retain their provenance.

Google/Microsoft/Okta identity, HRIS connections, Slack/Teams apps, mobile app
stores, mail, video, model and licensed-content accounts require employer
authorization, provider permissions and any necessary agreements. Beam shows
what is connected, unavailable, failed or awaiting approval. Provider
subscriptions and content licenses are separately disclosed in USD. A buyer
may choose native package support or an authorized player/LRS connection; both
must meet the same evidence and learner outcomes. Beam does not build a new
Zoom, video CDN, payroll product or library publisher to obtain those services.

Current local file admissions (25 MB source, 8 MB practical evidence and
10 MB per import file with five TalentLMS course reports) are implementation
limits, not target capacity. Larger media/packages and 200-employee operation
