# LTI 1.3 platform (preview)

The owner registers a tool with `POST /api/beam/lti.register`. Supply `name`, the exact installation `issuer` (the app origin), a unique `clientId`, `deployment`, exact HTTPS `loginUrl` and `redirectUri`, `consent: true`, and `jwks: {keys: [...]}` containing public RSA signature keys with unique `kid`, `alg: RS256`, `use: sig`, exponent AQAB and a 2048–8192-bit modulus. Private keys and token-controlled key URLs are refused. The server operator must first list the trusted tool hostname in comma-separated `BEAM_LTI_HOSTS`; the default is empty. The login and redirect URLs share one admitted origin. No remote JWK lookup or arbitrary server-side URL fetch occurs.

Registration returns the platform issuer, authorization, JWKS and token endpoints. The platform's private signing key is sealed under the installation's existing server-only session-secret root. Its public JWKS is at `/api/beam/lti/jwks`. Losing that root requires platform key rotation.

The owner posts `lti.deep-link` with `tool` and `course`, then opens the returned `loginUrl`. The tool completes ordinary LTI OIDC against `/api/beam/lti/authorize` using the returned login/message hints, its exact registered redirect, `response_type=id_token`, `response_mode=form_post`, `scope=openid`, `prompt=none`, `state` and `nonce`. The preparing human must still be signed in; the hint expires after five minutes and can authorize once. The platform's consent handoff posts `id_token` and `state` to the exact redirect after the person presses Continue. It never follows a tool-supplied redirect.

A deep-linking response posts `JWT` to `/api/beam/lti/deep-link` without a human session cookie. It must be RS256-signed by a pinned current tool key, issuer equal to its registered client id, audience equal to the platform issuer, LTI version 1.3.0, exact deployment, message type LtiDeepLinkingResponse and the original deep-linking `data`. One ltiResourceLink item is accepted, on the tool's admitted origin. Its title and URL are data. Expiry, one-use data and signature checks reject replay or foreign results. Selection does not publish or mutate lessons.

After review, the owner posts `lti.attach` with `resource`, `course`, `draftVersion`, `lesson` and `passPercent` (1–100). This adds a required external exercise to the editable draft. Submit and publish through Beam's existing named human reviewer. A published lesson/revision is never modified by a callback. Copies need an owner-approved resource for the copied course.

On the assigned learner's course overview, Open tool initiates `lti.launch` with `assignment` and `resource`. A live assignment, current access window and met prerequisite are required again at authorization. The signed LtiResourceLinkRequest includes the exact resource, a tool-specific opaque subject, course context, Learner role and an assignment-bound AGS lineitem. No name, email, answers, evidence or private practice is included.

To submit AGS scores, the tool posts client_credentials to `/api/beam/lti/token` with `client_id`, `client_assertion_type=urn:ietf:params:oauth:client-assertion-type:jwt-bearer`, a signed `client_assertion` and score-only scope `https://purl.imsglobal.org/spec/lti-ags/scope/score`. The assertion issuer and subject equal its client id, audience is the exact token endpoint, lifetime is at most five minutes, and `jti` must be new. The opaque token expires in five minutes. Protocol callbacks refuse any session cookie; ordinary session credentials cannot become integration grants.

POST to the launch's `lineitem + /scores` with that bearer grant and AGS score fields: `userId` exactly the launch subject, `scoreGiven`, positive `scoreMaximum`, `activityProgress`, `gradingProgress` and a new ISO `timestamp`. Foreign lineitems/users, nonfinite or out-of-range scores, duplicate/older timestamps, closed/replaced assignments and revoked deployments are refused. Only Completed + FullyGraded at the reviewed threshold satisfies that external requirement. The result never substitutes for native lessons, quiz, practical or attendance; normal completion retains exact result/resource/revision lineage. Incomplete and failing results remain evidence without becoming completion or competence.

`lti.keys` with `tool`, current `version` and public `jwks` replaces the tool keys and invalidates its outstanding bearer grants. `lti.platform.rotate` rotates the platform key; retired public keys remain in JWKS for ten minutes to verify unexpired launches. `lti.revoke` ends a deployment and its grants immediately while preserving earlier completion evidence. Owner-only `GET /api/beam/lti` exposes registration/resource/result lineage, never private signing material or bearer tokens.

Archives withhold the private platform key and exclude ephemeral AGS tokens. Restore revokes deployments and consumes pending launches; the owner must register a fresh deployment/client and rotate platform keys before new launches. Retention, isolated restore recovery and complete real-tool interoperability remain follow-up qualification. The feature stays partial pending ct approval of its minimal-data consent and grade defaults.
