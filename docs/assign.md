Managers and the owner assign training on **Manage** and follow it up from the same page.

## Assign a course

Under **Assign a course**, choose the **Course**, tick **People** or whole **Teams**, and set the **Due date**, **Due time** and **Time zone**. Press **Assign**. People who already have an open assignment for the course keep it.

Every assignment names the course revision that was published when it was made.

## Role paths

A role path lists the courses a team needs, in order. Open **Paths**, use **New path** to name it and pick its team, then **Edit requirements**: for each course, choose an optional **Prerequisite**, the **Days to complete** and how often to **Renew every (days)**. **Save as new revision** keeps earlier assignments on the path revision they came from.

**Assign to the team** gives every member of the team the path's courses. A course behind a prerequisite stays locked until the prerequisite is complete.

## The queues

The top of **Manage** shows what needs you:

- **Overdue**: open assignments past their due time.
- **Out of quiz attempts**: people who used every attempt without passing. Write a **Reason for another attempt** and press **Grant one more attempt**.
- **Renewals due**: completions that expire within 30 days. **Assign all renewals** assigns each one again.

## Record offline training

For training held in person, use **Record offline training**: choose the **Course** and the **Attested revision**, tick the **Attendees**, and fill in **Held on**, **Instructor**, **Location** and the **Attestation**. Attach a signed attendance sheet if you have one. Each attendee gets a completion and a certificate for that revision. Nobody can attest their own attendance; ask another manager or the owner.

## Learner records

**Learner records** lists everyone. Open a person to see their assignments, completions with certificates, and any [imported history](/docs/move-in).
