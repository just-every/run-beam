A course in Beam is built from your own procedures. Every lesson cites the exact version of the documents it teaches, so when a document changes, Beam knows which courses to look at again.

## Add a source

A source is a procedure or other material your business owns or may use: a handbook, a checklist, a short video. Open **Sources** and use **Add a source**:

- **Title** and **File**.
- **License**: owned by the business, licensed to the business, or used with written permission, with the **license evidence**.
- **Where it came from**, so anyone can trace it.
- The **responsible reviewer**.

Every version is kept unchanged. When the document changes, open it and use **Upload a new version**. Courses that cite the earlier version are flagged for their reviewer (see [Review and publish](/docs/review#when-a-source-changes)).

## Create a course

Open **Courses** and use **New course**: give it a **Title**, an optional **Summary**, and the **Reviewer who publishes it**. You land on the course's draft.

### Lessons

Use **Add a lesson** with a **Lesson title** and **Lesson text**, and tick the source versions it **cites**. Lesson text is plain text with a little structure:

- A blank line starts a new paragraph.
- Lines starting with `- ` make a list, and lines starting with `1. `, `2. ` and so on make numbered steps.
- A line starting with `# ` is a heading.
- When a lesson opens with a one-line paragraph, Beam highlights it for the learner: put the line that matters first.

Open a lesson in the draft to edit it, **Move up**, or **Remove lesson**.

### Quiz

Use **Add a question**: write the **Question**, the **Choices, one per line**, and the **Correct choice numbers**, such as `2`, or `1,3` when several are correct. A question scores only when exactly the right choices are picked, so scoring is the same every time.

Under **Add a question** you can also pick **Fill in the blank** (write `___` where each blank goes, then one line of **Accepted answers** per blank with alternatives separated by `|`, and tick **Ignore upper and lower case** if you want; spaces at either end never count), **Ordering** (the items one per line in the right order) or **Matching** (one `left = right` pair per line). Learners fill each blank, choose an item for each position or a match for each item from labelled lists, and a question scores only when every blank, position or pair is right.

### Assessment rules

Under **Assessment rules**, set the **Pass mark (%)** and the number of **Quiz attempts allowed**. For practical work, write the **Practical assessment instructions** and choose the **Named assessor** who decides the evidence. Leave the instructions empty for a course without a practical part.

## Send for review

**Submit for review** freezes the draft as a new revision for the course's reviewer. You can keep editing the draft; the revision in review does not change. The reviewer publishes it or returns it with a note: see [Review and publish](/docs/review).

## Retire or copy a course

On a course's page, **Retire this course** with a reason when it should no longer be assigned, for example when a newer course replaces it. Nobody can assign it again, through Manage, a team, a role path or a renewal, until you press **Reinstate for new assignments**; people who already have it can still finish it, and its records, certificates and reports stay. **Copy into a new course** makes an unpublished course with the same lessons, quiz and reviewer, ready to edit and send for review.
