Only a course's named reviewer publishes its revisions. A published revision never changes, and every completion stays bound to the revision it was made against.

## Publish or return a revision

Revisions waiting for you appear under **Waiting for my review** on **My learning**. Open one to read every lesson and the assessment as learners will see them.

To publish, choose what happens **When published**:

- **Keep current assignments on their revision**: people finish the revision they started.
- **Move open assignments to this revision**: people still working move to the new revision.
- **Also reassign everyone who completed an earlier revision**: everyone takes the new revision, including those already done.

Set the **Days to complete a reassignment**, add a note if you like, and press **Publish this revision**.

To send it back instead, write **What needs changing** and press **Return to author**. Earlier completions stay bound to the revision they were made against.

## When a source changes

When an author uploads a new version of a source, every course whose published lessons cite the earlier version shows **Source changed** to its reviewer, naming the old and new version numbers. Read the change and record your decision:

- **No material change to the training**: the course stays as it is.
- **Training needs updating (the draft is being revised)**: the author updates the lessons and submits a new revision, which you publish with the reassignment you choose.

## Proposed changes

An author's agent can propose changes to a course draft, with the source passages it relies on and anything it could not find. They appear on the course page as **Proposed change**: **Apply to draft** or **Decline**. Applying changes only the draft; nothing is published until a reviewer publishes a revision.
