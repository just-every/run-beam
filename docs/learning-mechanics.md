OPS-001/004, PACK-003/004/005, AUTHOR-009, LEARN-004/005/008 and future connector/index/native-cache doors inherit these custody and erasure obligations.

## OPS-004

Owners schedule encrypted backups of learning data and files, choose retention/location, see last successful backup and test recovery into an isolated installation. A restored copy starts with automation and provider delivery disabled until reviewed, and validates schema/digests/erasure fences before activation.

## OPS-006

The employer can install, configure, upgrade, build, deploy, diagnose, export and restore Beam through documented rer commands in its own supported Cloudflare account. Native local learning works without provider accounts; provider-dependent features explicitly require real connections and never simulate a delivered message, model answer or course result.

## OPS-007

Identity, mail, dependencies, data transport, import custody and portability use declared Base interfaces or a replacement that proves the same public-door guarantees. Account ownership, revocation, session isolation, safe configuration diagnostics, source integrity and archive exclusion of secrets remain required after any replacement; pinned-copy verification stays enabled. Archive exclusion of secrets means the installation archive `rer export` writes carries no verifier: the hashes of application credential secrets, certificate verification link tokens and restricted report link keys are replaced by `withheld` (`withheld:<id>` where the column is unique), while every other column of those grants (who issued, scoped, rotated or revoked them, and when) travels as decision lineage; a restore marks each such grant it brings back restored and disabled until the owner reissues it (rotating a credential reissues its secret; a restored link never works again and a new link replaces it), including grants from an archive written before this rule, which still restores. Qualification covers every capability that exists today. Each future capability or component replacement inherits these same public-door guarantees before it ships, including the new identity, native, provider and importer doors in ORG-011, LEARN-004–005, CONNECT-018, NOTICE-002–003, MOVE-001–007 and OPS-003–006; the baseline and candidate repeat the applicable command, HTTP, file and custody outcomes as well as copy/source checks.


## ORG-011

Frontline staff with no company mailbox are full Beam learners. A person can exist with no email address, identified by their employee id (ORG-001), whether added by hand, by roster upload (ORG-006), by a file feed (CONNECT-019) or by the HRIS (AUTO-002). The owner switches each sign-in method on or off: an admin-issued username with a password or a numeric PIN of at least six digits, which the person sets on first use from a one-time initial secret; and a one-time code sent by SMS to a verified mobile number from the roster, through the employer’s SMS provider such as Twilio. Both methods work on the web and in the native apps (LEARN-004), and open assignment links from SMS and in-app notices (NOTICE-001, NOTICE-005, LEARN-012). An admin, or a manager for their own team (ORG-004), resets a forgotten password or PIN by issuing a new one-time initial secret; each reset ends the person’s sessions and records who reset whom and when (OPS-001). Failed attempts are rate limited per person and per device; repeated failures lock the account until a cool-down passes or an admin or manager unlocks it. SMS codes expire after 10 minutes by default, which the owner can set between 2 and 15 minutes, and work once. Deactivation, removed access and AUTO-002 leavers end every web and app session, as BEAM-01 does for emailed sign-in. Passwords and PINs are stored only as salted slow hashes, are never shown after issue, and never appear in exports (BEAM-16, OPS-002) or agent reads. Each new hire gets a printable first-sign-in sheet in their LEARN-017 language with the portal address, username, a QR code that opens sign-in with the username filled in, and the initial secret, which expires after a set number of days. A tablet the owner registers as a shared device, such as a break-room tablet, offers quick switching by username and PIN, signs the person out after inactivity or when they leave a course, remembers nobody and keeps no offline downloads (LEARN-005). A person without email never gets email: notices go in-app and by SMS where they have a verified number and have not opted out (LEARN-012); with neither route, the manager’s queue shows “no contact route” for that person (NOTICE-004), never a silent drop. The owner and learning administrators (ORG-002) keep emailed or workplace sign-in for owner recovery; learners, managers, assessors and instructors may use either method.

## PACK-006

Admins register LTI 1.3 tools with exact deployment, issuer and key configuration, authorize deep links and minimal learner data, and reconcile permitted Assignment and Grade Services results to the assigned revision. Revoked or foreign deployment results are rejected. Registration, deep-link authorization and threshold approval are available to the owner, a learning administrator within the course's unit, and a custom role holding both `integrations` and `courses` for that unit. Delegated registrations explicitly name that unit; legacy owner registrations stay installation-wide. Conservative default pending ct: team/location-only custom grants never grant course or tool authority; both permissions must reach the same explicit unit. Current registration authority and course-unit membership are rechecked at launch and callback, so ending a grant withholds results. Platform private-key creation and rotation remain owner-only. Registration needs explicit consent, the exact installation issuer/client/deployment, server-admitted HTTPS tool URLs and pinned public RS256 keys. Deep-link selection is one-use and signed; an external-tool block enters a draft only through that approval and only for a resource selected for the same course, and the normal human reviewer publishes. Learner launches send only a tool-specific pseudonymous subject, course context and protocol roles, never name/email/evidence; the pseudonym is not anonymity. AGS score-only grants expire after five minutes and each score is bound to the tool, learner, resource, assignment and exact revision. The authorized administrator selects a required grade threshold; incomplete or failing grades do not pass, and every native lesson/assessment/practical/attendance requirement still gates completion through tryComplete/recordCompletion. Deep-link consumption/resource creation and assertion/token issuance are each atomic. Concurrent duplicate scores store one result; the learner's explicit `lti.reconcile` action recovers a stored result after interrupted completion through the same native gates. Duplicate assertions/results, foreign users/tools and revoked deployments are refused; rotation drops outstanding service grants, and archived secrets/grants never reactivate integrations. Revocation and restore are terminal today: reconnecting needs a new registration, deep link, reviewed revision and move (AUTHOR-010), and open assignments requiring a revoked tool cannot complete until moved or excepted. OPS-003 inherits LTI lineage retention/erasure, OPS-004 the restore activation fence and AUTHOR-005 the approval of copied-course tool links (a copy leaves external-tool blocks out until the owner attaches them for it).

## LIB-006

Hourly employers track certificates people earn outside Beam, such as food-handler cards, forklift operator certification, CPR and first aid, and OSHA 10/30-hour cards. A learner, or their manager for them, uploads an external certificate with issuer, certificate number, issue and expiry dates and the certificate file or photo, against a requirement the employer defines (for example “Food handler card, renew on expiry”). Files are stored as verified, inert bytes like BEAM-02 sources. A reviewer the employer names for the requirement (manager, assessor or learning administrator) approves it or rejects it with a reason; nothing counts before approval, and Beam issues no Beam certificate for it. An approved certificate counts toward the requirement in REPORT-001, labeled external, until its expiry; AUTO-004 and NOTICE-001 remind the person and their manager before expiry and ask for the renewed certificate, and a lapsed one moves the person to missing. Rejections, replacements and expired certificates stay in the person’s transcript (REPORT-003) and export (BEAM-16). Requirements can be assigned by role and location through AUTO-001. The owner defines requirements (acting as the employer’s administrator) and assigns them to teams and to AUTO-001 audiences, so a requirement applies to whoever is in one of its teams or audiences now. The reviewer is the person’s managers, one named assessor, or the learning administrators of the person’s units; the owner reviews for anyone, and nobody reviews their own certificate. The person uploads their own certificate, or one of their managers (or the owner) uploads it for them. Files are JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF photos or PDFs, downloaded only as inert attachments by the person, the reviewer and whoever may read the person’s record. An approved certificate is current through the end of its expiry date in the business time zone and lapses at the next local midnight; a requirement may instead say its certificates do not expire. The renewal reminder starts at local midnight the requirement’s number of days before that lapse and shows in the app until a renewed certificate is approved: to the person on My learning and External certificates, and to their managers on External certificates. Beam does not email these reminders yet: delivered email reminders are NOTICE-001’s, which carries them. REPORT-001 counts an approved, unexpired certificate as completed (expiring within 30 days, as for courses) with the kind “external certificate”, and every person the requirement applies to without one as missing.

## NOTICE-001

Using the employer’s configured mail provider such as Resend or Amazon SES, scheduled reminders deliver assignment, due, overdue and renewal messages in the learner’s time zone, state training is paid work time, and link to the exact requirement. Completion, cancellation and opted policy suppress obsolete messages; accepted, delivered, bounced and rejected states are distinct. A person with no email (ORG-011) gets the same reminders in-app and by SMS where they have a verified number and consent; otherwise their manager sees “no contact route” (NOTICE-004). These four learner reminders are the defaults; admins add, retarget and reword notifications under NOTICE-005, and the same suppression and delivery states apply there. Training mail also delivers LIVE-001's calendar invitations: a learner who chooses a session, and each registered learner when it is rescheduled, changed or canceled, receives it as a mailed iCalendar invitation carrying the same event UIDs and sequence as Beam's calendar file, under the same suppression and delivery states; a LIVE-002 seat offer (with its expiry) and a move from a waitlist into a session are mailed the same way. LIB-006’s renewal reminders for external certificates are mailed the same way to the person and their managers from the requirement’s reminder day (until then Beam shows them only in the app). Before unattended provider calls ship, this clause inherits AUTO-008's policy/approval/scope, quiet hours, rate/spend ceiling and kill switch; its acceptance must deny a provider call, inspect the recorded failure and replay safely without hidden retries. NOTICE-006 announcement delivery inherits consent, opt-out, quiet hours and delivery states through this clause, with no instructor exceptions; opted-out learners receive announcements in-app only, and the delivery state says so.

## CONNECT-017

Write-back is optional per connected HRIS and per course. When on, each revision-bound completion, including every recompletion and renewal, is written once to the person’s HRIS training record under a reviewed mapping from the Beam course or path to the HRIS training type, with completion date, hours, and certificate number or link. One completion is one HRIS record, so a renewal adds a second record rather than overwriting the first. BambooHR is required (its employee training-record API). Willow, Rippling, ADP Workforce Now and the other connected HRIS get the same where their API offers a training or learning record; where an HRIS API offers none, the Integrations page says so. On the first sync Beam reads the HRIS’s existing completed trainings and, through a reviewed mapping of HRIS training types to Beam courses (not exact-name matching), imports them as labeled HRIS history: it shows in transcripts and reports but never closes a current assignment, matching BEAM-15’s import rule, and counts toward a current requirement only through a MOVE-009 credit decision. Each write has a delivery state; failures, permission loss and a deleted HRIS training type are visible and retried safely without duplicate records. Beam never edits or deletes HRIS records it did not write.

## MOVE-009

People trained in the incumbent are not retrained on cutover day, and the compliance dashboard is right from the first sync. In the MOVE-008 cutover step a learning administrator (ORG-002), or the qualified reviewer named for a LIB-002 requirement, approves one credit decision per mapped course or requirement, with a written reason and the renewal interval taken from the requirement (AUTO-004, LIB-002), which the decision may set where the requirement has none. One decision covers every matched imported completion for that mapping from any importer (BEAM-15, MOVE-001 to MOVE-007) and from HRIS training history imported under CONNECT-017; a later import into the same mapping is previewed and added only on a fresh approval. Before approval a write-free preview lists who will be credited and names each exclusion with its reason: a completion older than the interval (already expired), an unmatched person, a different course or edition from the requirement (for example a non-supervisor course for a California supervisor requirement), and a person who already holds a current native completion, which always wins. Where one person has several imported completions the latest counts. Each credited person is shown as “current, credited from TalentLMS history” (or the source named) until the imported certificate expiry where the import carries one, otherwise the historical completion date plus the interval, in the employer’s time zone. REPORT-001 counts credited people as current, labeled as credited and moving to expiring and then missing on that date. AUTO-004 schedules each credited person’s renewal from that expiry: the renewal assignment is created at the rule’s lead time and is due on the credited expiry. AUTO-002 and LIB-002 create no new assignment for a requirement a valid credit covers; an assignment already open for a credited person is listed in the preview and stays open unless a manager cancels it under AUTO-006 with the credit as the reason. The imported row stays labeled history, as BEAM-15 requires: Beam issues no native completion or certificate for it, and the original certificate PDF imported under MOVE-001 is attached to the credit where supplied. Learners see their credited status and renewal date on My learning; transcripts and exports (REPORT-003, BEAM-16) show the credit with its decision, reviewer, reason and source row; the decision and its undo are recorded in OPS-001. Undoing a credit decision returns each person it covered to missing, or to open where an assignment exists, and changes no imported row, assignment, attempt, completion or certificate. Agents may draft a mapping (AGENT-007) but never approve or undo a credit.

## LEARN-019

An admin, or a manager for their own team, resets one learner’s open assignment, giving a reason. Until delegated admins (ORG-002) and the manager hierarchy (ORG-004) exist, the owner acts as admin and can reset anyone; a manager can reset only a person who shares one of the manager’s teams. With ORG-004, the same authorization check uses the manager’s teams and hierarchy. These are dependencies on ORG-002 and ORG-004. On reset: lesson progress starts again from the first lesson, any unfinished attempt is closed as reset, and the attempt allowance starts again. Every earlier lesson record, attempt, response and practical decision stays in the assignment’s history and transcript (REPORT-003), marked as before the reset with who reset it and why (OPS-001); the learner sees a notice. A reset never touches a completion or certificate: a completed assignment cannot be reset, and the manager instead creates a new assignment for the same course, leaving the old completion bound to its revision (BEAM-09).

## ORG-002

The owner controls installation, identity, providers, billing and administrator grants. A learning administrator manages the catalog, enrollment and reports within assigned business units, without gaining owner/provider authority; grants, expiry and revocation apply in every page, API and download. Within their units a learning administrator also manages team membership: they invite new people as learners into their units' teams and set those teams' members from people already in their units' teams. They never change a team outside their units, give a role other than learner, or draw in someone from outside their units; a person they remove from every team of their units returns to the owner, and an invitation they sent stops admitting once their grant ends. The access window of an enrollment a learning administrator created is theirs to set and extend (AUTO-009); course and path windows and other organization-wide access policy stay with the owner, and managers extend windows for people in their teams. The owner names team managers (ORG-004). Beam has no billing today, so the billing part of this clause holds vacuously; a future billing capability inherits it and stays the owner's.
